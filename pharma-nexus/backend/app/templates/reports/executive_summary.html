System Overview

Pharma Nexus is an AI-powered drug repurposing discovery engine that integrates data from 13 public biomedical databases to identify potential new uses for existing drugs against cancer. The system generates hypotheses by analyzing pathway overlap, gene expression patterns, literature evidence, clinical trial data, safety profiles, and novelty across all drug-cancer combinations, then uses Claude (Anthropic) to produce expert-level mechanistic narratives and critical assessments.
